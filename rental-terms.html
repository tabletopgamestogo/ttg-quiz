How It Works

  • Tell us if you're booking as an individual or for a company.
  • Be ready to submit a copy of your government ID — don't worry, we handle it under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).
  • Borrowers must be 18 years old or older.
  • A booking is confirmed once the down payment and security deposit are settled, and a signed Rental Agreement is on file with us.

Rental Periods

Choose a 1, 2, or 3-day package. Release time is 10:00 AM, with return due by 7:00 AM on the corresponding day.

  • 1 Day — return by 7:00 AM the following day.
  • 2 Days — return by 7:00 AM, two days after release.
  • 3 Days — return by 7:00 AM, three days after release.
Delivery time doesn't count against your rental time — if we arrive early, you can start right away. If renting multiple packages in one booking, each still returns on its own schedule, even if others in the same order run longer.

Payment & Security Deposit

A booking is confirmed once 75% of the Rental Fee plus a Security Deposit (equal to 50% of the item value) are paid. The remaining 25% of the Rental Fee is automatically deducted from the deposit after the rental concludes, and any remaining balance is refunded within 2–5 business days after we inspect the returned items.

We accept Cash, GCash, Maya, and Bank Transfer (BDO or BPI).

Taking Care of the Games

You'll have one hour after release to inspect the items and flag any pre-existing issues. After that, we ask that you:

  • Handle everything with reasonable care and keep components complete.
  • Never write on, mark, glue, paint, tear, cut, tape, or otherwise alter any piece.
  • Keep everything free of stains, food residue, bite marks, and liquid damage.
  • Keep sleeves, cases, and packaging intact.
  • Return everything in the same condition you received it, allowing for normal, reasonable wear.
  • Let us know right away if anything gets damaged, lost, or goes missing.

Late, Lost & Damaged Items

  • An item is considered late if it isn't packed within 15 minutes of the agreed return time.
  • An item is considered lost if it isn't returned within 24 hours of the agreed return time, and there's no communication from you about it.
  • Depending on the situation, charges may apply for replacing missing components, repairing damage, professional cleaning, or full replacement value (plus shipping) for anything that can't be restored.
  • We'll send an itemized statement for any charges, which is due within 7 days after the agreed return time — deducted from your security deposit first, with any remaining balance billed to you.

Bookings must be made 3 to 30 days ahead of your release date. If you need to cancel a confirmed booking:

If we need to cancel your confirmed booking, you'll get a full refund of your down payment and deposit — or, if you'd rather, we'll apply it toward a rescheduled booking.

Hiring a Game Master (Optional)

Want someone to run the game for you? Our Game Masters handle:

  • Setting up the game and any initial setup steps.
  • Teaching the rules at the start, with clarifications as you play.
  • Tracking score when the game calls for it.
  • Settling rules disputes as the final word at the table.
  • Keeping the pace of play moving.
  • Packing everything away once you're done playing that round, as long as the GM is still on the premises.

Hire a GM in 1 to 4-hour blocks, used consecutively at the start of your rental period — not spread across a multi-day rental. If we arrive late or a session ends early for reasons on our end, you can choose a time extension or a partial refund.

Good to Know

By booking with us, you consent to our collection and use of your name, address, contact details, and ID number for booking, verification, and service purposes, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). We may photograph or record your session for our marketing — social media, our website, print materials. If you'd rather not be included, just let us know in writing before your session and we'll do our best to leave you out of anything we publish.
